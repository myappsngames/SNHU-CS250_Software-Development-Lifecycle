## Reflection

#### How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?

* Intepreting user needs and translating them into a program is a crucial step in software development. Creating user stories helps interpret and implement user needs by providing clarity, prioritization, and focus. User stories provide a clear and concise understanding of user needs for developers. Additionally, user stories guide development efforts by being easily prioritized based on importance and user impact. User stories also keep the development process focused on delivering features that are more important to users.

#### How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?

* I approach developing programs by focusing on iterative development, prioritizng user needs, and embracing collaboration. In my future development work, I hope to incorporate the Scrum Framework and Test-Driven Development (TDD). In group projects the Scrum Framework will provide a flexible and structured approach that can keep the team focused and efficient. By implementing TDD, unit tests will ensure code quality and help identify bugs early in development. By incorporating Agile principles, I can develop programs that are more responsive to user needs, adaptable to change, and delivered with higher quality.
    
#### What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?

* In the world of software development, being a good team member goes beyond just writing clean code. A good team member communicates effectively with the team and clearly articulates ideas, progress updates, and roadbloacks. A good team member is not only adaptable, but also accountable. Ultimately, a good team member in software development is respectful and embraces collaboration.
